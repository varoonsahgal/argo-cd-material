# Course Quality Rubric

Use this rubric for the final review.

## Scope and alignment
- Matches the supplied outline.
- Covers every stated objective.
- Avoids uncontrolled scope creep.

## Technical correctness
- Concepts are accurate.
- Code is internally consistent.
- Current claims are source-checked when necessary.
- Deprecated patterns are avoided unless explicitly taught for comparison.

## Practicality
- Labs are runnable.
- Setup is realistic.
- Activities fit the available time.
- Compute and dependency requirements are sensible.

## Teaching quality
- Intuition precedes complexity.
- Examples are concrete.
- Visuals and demos clarify difficult ideas.
- Misconceptions are addressed.

## Engagement
- Predict-before-run activities are used naturally.
- Broken-model/debugging activities appear where useful.
- Before/after comparisons are visible.
- Competitive elements reward reasoning, not only raw scores.

## Consistency
- Terminology and notation are consistent across days.
- Files follow a predictable structure.
- Cross-references point to real files/sections.

## Completeness
- Lesson material, labs, assessments, takeaways, and instructor notes exist where appropriate.
- No placeholders remain unless explicitly marked as future work.


## Instructor Solutions
- Is every substantial lab paired with a separate instructor-only solution?
- Does every solution answer all participant tasks and explain the reasoning?
- Has the solution been executed end-to-end?
- Are expected outputs and realistic metric ranges documented?
- Are common misconceptions and recovery notes included?
- Is there zero solution leakage into participant-facing artifacts?
