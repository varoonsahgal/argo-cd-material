# Courseware Content Contract

Use this contract when generating course materials.

## Required outputs
The final courseware should normally contain:
- course blueprint and timing map
- lesson content for every outline section
- instructor talking points and teaching notes
- demonstrations and visual ideas
- labs or guided exercises where appropriate
- challenge activities and prediction exercises
- knowledge checks and scenario-based assessments
- key takeaways and transitions
- common misconceptions and troubleshooting notes
- real-world engineering connections
- final quality review

## Traceability
Each major outline objective should map to at least one instructional component and one opportunity to practice or demonstrate understanding.

Use identifiers when helpful:
- `OBJ-D2-03` objective
- `LESSON-D2-03` lesson
- `LAB-D2-02` lab
- `CHECK-D2-03` assessment

## Density rule
If the outline contains more material than can reasonably be delivered in the allotted time:
1. identify the overload explicitly,
2. preserve the highest-value learning path,
3. move optional depth into callouts or extension material,
4. do not silently compress everything into shallow bullet points.

## Writing rule
Courseware should be usable as teaching material, not merely a topic list. Expand enough to support delivery while avoiding textbook-style overproduction.
